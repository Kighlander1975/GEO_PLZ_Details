## Provenance policy

The original historical relation_source values
must not be silently overwritten.

Independent corroboration and historical origin
are different attributes.

A future public data release should record
both the source lineage and corroborating
evidence in its metadata.

The legal release status of the combined
database remains subject to separate review.
